The proof is on the shop's own screen.

The FingerPay terminal is the only FingerPay app, and it belongs to the shop. Shoppers never install anything. It runs in a browser today; an Android app comes next.

  • One line, no doubt

    Each result is one plain line, such as PAYMENT SUCCESSFUL — PKR 180 RECEIVED. There's no screenshot to inspect.

  • Today's ledger

    Every sale with its time, amount, fee, net and Raast reference (simulated). Shoppers appear as a first name and initial, and only on settled sales.

  • Fix a mistake the same day

    Typed PKR 1,800 instead of 180? The owner voids the sale with a reason. The shopper gets the money back and an SMS.

  • Export the day

    The owner downloads the day's settlement as a CSV for the accountant.

  • Private by design

    The shop never sees a shopper's balance, CNIC or phone number. On a decline, the screen says why and never who.

  • Offline is clear

    If the internet drops, the screen says TAKE CASH. Nothing is queued, so nothing gets charged later by surprise.

Lock your thumb with one word.

A thumb can't be changed like a PIN. So FingerPay assumes one will be copied someday, and limits what a copy can do.

LOCK
Simulated SMS

Thumb payments are now LOCKED. Visit any Allied Bank branch with your CNIC to unlock.

  • No fingerprint images

    No fingerprint image is stored, sent or logged. The database keeps only a keyed code (an HMAC), made with a key that never enters the database. A copy of the database alone holds no usable thumbs and no readable CNICs.

  • Thumb Lock by SMS

    Reply LOCK to any FingerPay SMS and thumb payments stop. Only a bank branch can unlock them, with the shopper's CNIC.

    Why: in India, fraudsters have lifted thumbprints from public property records, cast them in silicone and emptied bank accounts. The defence police there recommend most is a biometric lock the owner controls. Sources: Deccan Herald, Gujarat Cyber Crime, Scroll.

  • An alert on every attempt

    The shopper gets an SMS for every payment, every declined attempt with their thumb, and any try on a locked thumb. Misuse shows up the same day, not on next month's statement.

  • Retries never charge twice

    Each sale is one charge on the server. If the network stutters and the terminal asks again, the server replays the first receipt instead of charging again. Paying the same shop the same amount again within 15 seconds is blocked.

  • Limits

    A thumb pays at most PKR 5,000 a tap on a salary account and PKR 2,000 on a student account, with daily caps of PKR 15,000 and PKR 5,000. Each sale allows 3 tries, and 10 unknown thumbs in 10 minutes pauses the terminal.

A declined sale costs PKR 0. Every refund needs two different bank staff. Every change is written to a tamper-evident audit log.

What a real launch still needs

  • A certified scanner with liveness detection, tested to ISO/IEC 30107-3, with a false-match rate of 1 in 10,000 or better.
  • Above about 1,000 shoppers, matching by thumb alone gets less reliable. Production adds a second check that stays phone-free: the last 4 digits of a CNIC or phone typed at the till, or a second finger.

No app, no data plan, no smartphone.

Paying needs no phone at all. Everything after the sale comes by plain SMS, on any basic phone with SMS: no smartphone, no data plan.

The receipt, decline alerts, Thumb Lock and disputes all arrive as text. To lock thumb payments, reply LOCK. To question a payment, reply DISPUTE to its receipt.
