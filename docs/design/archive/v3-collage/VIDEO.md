# FingerPay video system

Covers FingerPay's video style rules and the two videos the website and the pitch need:
1. **Hero loop:** 12 s, silent. The website hero's poster and fallback, and a social clip.
2. **Pitch film:** about 84 s, narrated, for competition judges and investors.

> **Inputs:**
> - `docs/design/BRIEF.md` (binding, including the 2026-11-18 *Direction decision: ILLUSTRATED*)
> - **`docs/design/ART-PROMPTS.md`**: the cast, the style block and the vignettes. The films are built from those generated assets.
> - `PRODUCT.md`, `docs/SPEC.md` §1, §5 View A and §6.3
> - the SMS text in `supabase/migrations/20261118000300_fp_api.sql`
> - `brand/fingerpay-logo-source.png`
>
> **Depends on docs still being written:**
> - `TYPE-AND-COLOR.md`: UI hexes and fonts. This doc only names colour *roles*.
> - `MOTION.md`: frame-player and UI timing.
> - `APP.md`: View A, B and E screens.
> - `WEBSITE.md`: how the vignettes sit on the page.
> - `brand/BRAND.md`: the logo vectors.
>
> Where this doc and those disagree, those win on their own subject, and this doc gets updated.
> **Status:** plan only. Nothing is rendered.

## 0. The one rule

**The cast is drawn. The product is real.**

| Layer | What it is | Frame rate | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Illustrated world** | The vignettes and sheets from `ART-PROMPTS.md`: Rasheed, Ayesha, Bilal, the counter, the scanner pad, the phone on its stand, the keypad phone | 12 fps (the processed sequences the site uses) | Generated by the user in Google Flow or Higgsfield, then cut out and colour-corrected |
| **Product inserts** | The real merchant app UI (View A, B, E), the real SMS text, the real test output, captions, the logo | 24 fps | The app's own components, a recorded demo run (§4.2), and the brand vectors |

**The art never contains text.** That is already a rejection rule in `ART-PROMPTS.md`. Every word, number and screen in a video is added in code over a blank screen in the art:
- the blank phone-on-stand
- the "For shops" white rectangle
- the blank keypad phone

That keeps the two layers apart. The drawings carry the story. Every checkable claim sits on a real product surface.

## 1. Video brand system

### 1.1 Sources and what is fixed

| Element | Source | Fixed or chosen |
|---|---|---|
| Mark (nested fingerprint arches, open at the bottom, flat stroke ends at staggered lengths) | `brand/BRAND.md` SVG. The PNG is reference only | Fixed. Never redrawn or generated |
| Wordmark "FingerPay" | Brand vector artwork | Fixed. Never retyped: SF Pro's licence doesn't cover this use |
| Tagline "Making payments with one tap." | Brand | Fixed, in the `muted` role |
| Cast, props, illustration palette, line style | `ART-PROMPTS.md` §1 style block and §2 sheets, plus the approved generations | Fixed. Video uses the same processed files as the site |
| UI states, labels, banner copy, timing | App components and `APP.md`; banner copy from SPEC §5 | Fixed. Video never retimes a UI animation |
| Money values, SMS text, references | The recorded demo run, checked against SPEC §6.1 and §6.3 | Fixed |
| Edit, camera, captions, ground, sound | This doc | Chosen |

### 1.2 Framing and camera

- **Ratios:** 16:9 is the master. Each other ratio is laid out again from layers, never cropped from the master:
  - 9:16 (1080×1920)
  - 4:5 (1080×1350)
  - 1:1 only if a destination asks for it
- **Use the vignette's own composition.** In the hero, Ayesha is on the left, Rasheed on the right, with headline space above. Asymmetry comes from where the product inserts sit: in the open third, at a different scale from the cast. The merchant screen is always the largest product surface.
- **Camera:** the vignettes are generated with a locked camera, so every move is done in post.
  - **Allowed:** 2D pushes and pans over the cut-out vignette layer, the `paper` ground and the inserts, with parallax of 0.3–0.6× between ground and vignette.
  - **Zoom on art:** 1.15× at most on 1080p sources. Up to 1.25× only if the generation is at least 1.25× the delivery size. Ask the generator for its largest size.
  - **Zoom on UI:** inserts render at 2× and can push to 2×.
  - **One move per beat**, then settle before the next action.
- **Cuts:** cut between the wide vignette and a prop close-up (from the §2d prop sheet: phone on stand, keypad phone) at the moment of the action, matching the art's key pose.
- **Readable UI:**
  - Inserts sit on the front-view props, or on the "For shops" screen, which faces the viewer.
  - The banner's cap height is at least 3.5% of the frame height.
  - Angled screens appear only in passing.
- **Safe areas:**
  - 16:9: 96 px left and right, 54 px top and bottom at 1920×1080.
  - 9:16: keep text and UI out of the top 250 px, the bottom 380 px and 60 px at each side.
- **Never:** Dutch angles, shake, whip-pans, zoom blur, lens flares, glitch effects, or any effect the style block forbids (glow, gradients, bloom, lens blur).

### 1.3 Illustration rules in video

- **Style:** the style block is the only definition of the look. Video adds nothing to the art:
  - no extra glow, light pass, grain on the characters, or 3D
  - the scanner's soft glow is already drawn in the vignette and the 6-stage thumb strip (`ART-PROMPTS.md` §4)
- **Quality gate:** use only generations that pass the rejection list in `ART-PROMPTS.md`. That means the same faces and clothes as the sheet, correct hands, no text, logos, flags or religious symbols, and a flat background.
- **Ground:** cut-outs sit on the `paper` role. `TYPE-AND-COLOR.md` should harmonise that role with the style block's warm paper.
- **The cast:** fictional personas (Rasheed Ahmed, Ayesha Khan, Bilal Hussain). Respectful, no caricature. Exactly as drawn on the approved sheets.

### 1.4 Frame rate

- **Timeline:** every video is cut at **24 fps**.
  - Art plays from the site's processed **12 fps** sequences: every second frame of the 24 fps generation, each held 2 timeline frames.
  - UI inserts, camera moves and captions update every frame.
  - 24 is the delivery rate that 12 fps divides evenly into. At 30 fps, art frames would land on 2.5-frame holds and judder.
- **Frame index:** art frame = `floor(t × 12) mod loopLength`, computed from video time. Renders are repeatable.
- **Screen captures:** recorded at 60 fps and conformed to 24 fps. The sale itself is never sped up (§2.3).

### 1.5 Motion language

Values map to `MOTION.md` tokens once that doc exists.

| Motion | Value |
|---|---|
| Signature: the mark reads a thumb (UI side) | On the terminal insert, the arches fill one at a time, inner to outer, 2 frames at 12 fps each. Built from the per-arch SVG paths. It lines up with the "sensor glowing" pose in the art |
| Thumb press (art) | The drawn sequence from the vignette or the §4 strip. Never redrawn or retimed |
| UI state changes | The app's real timing. Never shortened |
| Camera move | 600–900 ms, critically damped, equivalent to `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` |
| Caption in / out | 250 ms fade with a 2%-of-frame rise / 180 ms fade |
| Text hold | At least 1.2 s plus 0.3 s per word |
| Cuts | On the art's key pose. A dissolve (8 frames) only to show time passing |
| Idle | Hold a vignette's loop on its idle stretch. Blinks are already in the art |

### 1.6 Colour roles

There are two palettes, and each stays in its own layer:

| Palette | Owner | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Illustration palette (chai brown, turmeric, chilli red, bottle green, tarp blue, warm paper, ink `#1C1C1F`) | `ART-PROMPTS.md` §1 | The art only. Never sampled for UI, captions or titles |
| UI and brand roles | `TYPE-AND-COLOR.md` | Everything added in code |

UI and brand roles in video:

| Role | Use |
|---|---|
| `paper` | Ground and end card. Never `#fff` |
| `ink` | Mark, wordmark, primary text. Never `#000` |
| `muted` | Tagline, secondary text, caption metadata |
| `surface` | Caption plates, SMS panel |
| `accent` (at most one, ≤ 80% saturation, or none) | One focus cue per beat at most, such as the ring on the tapped preset |
| `success`, `decline`, `offline` | **Only inside the product UI**, exactly as the app renders them. Never as washes, glows or caption colours |

### 1.7 Type on screen

- **Fonts:** the `TYPE-AND-COLOR.md` families, with a licence that allows video embedding (OFL fonts do).
  - display role: titles and the end card
  - text role: captions and labels
  - tabular numbers for every amount
- **Minimum sizes at delivery resolution:**

| Format | Caption | Label | Title |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 16:9, 1920×1080 | 48 px | 40 px | 96–144 px |
| 4:5, 1080×1350 | 52 px | 44 px | 104–140 px |
| 9:16, 1080×1920 | 56 px | 48 px | 120–160 px |

- **Captions:**
  - at most 2 lines: 42 characters per line in 16:9, 32 in 9:16
  - semibold, `ink` on a `paper` plate at 92% opacity, `radius-md`
  - bottom centre inside the safe area
  - they follow the voiceover word for word
- **Copy rules:**
  - Banner and SMS text exactly as the product prints it. Words may be highlighted, but never edited.
  - "Risk Engine", never "AI".
  - No partner logos.
  - No invented numbers.

### 1.8 Sound

| Role | Rule |
|---|---|
| Voice | Leads the pitch film. Delivered at −16 LUFS integrated for the website and at −14 LUFS for uploads, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP. Recorded at 48 kHz / 24-bit |
| UI sounds | Three at most, each only on a visible action: keypad tick, thumb contact (on the art's "touching" pose), settle (a two-note tone, about 250 ms). If SPEC §13 #4 (the spoken Urdu confirmation, *"ek sau assi rupay wusool"*) is built, it replaces the settle tone |
| Generator audio | Thrown away. Strip any audio that Flow or Higgsfield adds |
| Music | Optional, sparse, owned or licensed, with the licence recorded. Mixed 20–24 LU under the voice |
| Silence | The hero loop has **no audio track**. The pitch film has 0.5 s of silence before the end card |

### 1.9 Covers and end card

- **End card** (pitch film):
  - the **Footer vignette**: Rasheed at dusk pulling the shutter halfway, waving
  - the lockup on its left on `paper`: mark above wordmark, tagline below, with clear space from `BRAND.md`
  - the full disclaimer underneath at caption size or larger
  - held at least 6 s
- **Hero loop poster:** frame 0 (§2.2), with no text. One per ratio, as AVIF and WebP:
  - ≤ 60 KB for desktop
  - ≤ 40 KB for mobile
  - It is the LCP element.
- **Pitch film cover:**
  - 1920×1080 plus 1280×720
  - the hero vignette's key frame (Ayesha's thumb on the pad, Rasheed behind), with the real success banner on a phone-on-stand insert in the open space
  - the only text is the tagline
  - checked at 320 px wide
- **Animated cover** (optional): the press-to-banner stretch, a 2 s loop, ≤ 500 KB. Watch 5 repeats to check the seam.

### 1.10 Export rules

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Master | 24 fps. ProRes 422 HQ or a PNG sequence at 2× the delivery size (art scaled no more than §1.2 allows). Rec.709 / sRGB |
| Website hero loop | MP4 AV1 first, MP4 H.264 High as fallback. No audio track, `+faststart`. 16:9 at 1920×1080 ≤ 2.5 MB; 4:5 or 9:16 for mobile ≤ 1.5 MB |
| Pitch film, website | MP4 H.264 1080p24 with AAC 48 kHz 192 kbps, ≤ 25 MB, `preload="none"`, WebVTT captions, transcript on the page |
| Pitch film, upload or submission | H.264 1080p24 at a high bitrate, plus SRT captions and a burned-in-caption version |
| Banding | Add grain only to large flat `paper` areas, never to the art |
| Names | `fingerpay-<video>_<ratio>_<res><fps>_v<nn>.<ext>` |
| Storage | Masters, raw generations and raw captures are kept **out of git** |

**Website embedding** (for `WEBSITE.md`):
- **Self-hosted only.** The CSP (SPEC §4.6) is `default-src 'self'` with no `frame-src`, so YouTube or Vimeo embeds would be blocked.
- **Autoplay:** `muted playsinline loop`, only without `prefers-reduced-motion` and with Save-Data off. Otherwise show the poster with a play button.
- **Visible pause button** (WCAG 2.2.2).
- Pause the video when it scrolls out of view.

### 1.11 QA before any delivery

1. Watch muted. The hero loop must make sense with no words.
2. Read every string at phone size, at 375 pt width.
3. Diff every string and spoken claim against the claims ledger (§3.3).
4. Check the art against the `ART-PROMPTS.md` rejection list. Faces and clothes must match the sheets across every vignette used.
5. **Privacy:**
   - no shopper name on a decline
   - no shopper balance on a merchant screen or in the terminal's frame region
   - first name and initial only
   - phone and CNIC last 4 only
   - no tokens, keys, CNICs, account numbers, URL bars, devtools, login screens or credential files in any frame
6. Check the loop seam over 5 repeats.
7. Measure loudness. Check that captions match the audio.
8. Compare exports side by side for drift.

## 2. Hero loop (silent, 12.0 s)

### 2.1 Purpose, assets, formats

- **Website:** the hero's poster and its fallback.
  - With reduced motion: the poster only.
  - With Save-Data or a failed scene load: the loop.
- **Social:** 4:5 and 9:16, with a persistent bottom line "Student prototype · integrations simulated". The exact disclaimer goes in the post text.
- **Art used** (all from `ART-PROMPTS.md`, nothing new):
  - Hero vignette (the 6 s loop)
  - §2d prop 2 (phone on stand, front view, blank)
  - How-it-works step 4: Rasheed smiling, a hand with a keypad phone buzzing
  - §2d prop 6 (keypad phone, front view, blank)
- **Persona:** Ayesha, the canonical demo sale in SPEC §6.3. The hero vignette shows her with no phone, so the receipt beat uses the anonymous hand from step 4. Bilal's variant is open question 4.

### 2.2 Beat sheet

- **Timeline:** 24 fps, 288 frames. Frame 0 is the poster and equals the end state.
- **Times follow the generated vignette:** "press" means the vignette's touching pose, wherever it falls. The cuts are aligned to it.
- **Latency `L`:** the real time from tap to banner, observed in the recorded run. The sheet assumes 1.0 s. If `L` is up to 2.0 s, shorten B6's hold. Above that, extend the loop to 14 s. **Never make `L` shorter than it really was.**

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B0 Settled (poster) | 0.00–1.20 | Hero vignette, end pose: Ayesha holding the bread and milk and smiling, Rasheed after his nod | Floating beside Rasheed in the open space: a phone-on-stand prop showing **PAYMENT SUCCESSFUL — PKR 180 RECEIVED** | Static wide | UI only |
| B1 New sale | 1.20–2.00 | Vignette loops back toward its start pose | Insert: `[Cancel / New Sale]` takes it to `IDLE` | Ease in, 1.00→1.08 | none |
| B2 Amount | 2.00–3.40 | **Cut** to the prop close-up (phone on stand, front) | `[PKR 180]` preset, the amount reads PKR 180, then `[Charge / Await Thumb Tap]` | Static close-up | none |
| B3 Waiting | 3.40–4.20 | **Cut** back to the wide hero vignette: Ayesha lifts her thumb | Small insert: `WAITING_FOR_THUMB`, with the waiting copy as `APP.md` defines it | Static | tag "Simulated" |
| B4 Press | 4.20–4.60 | Vignette: thumb on the pad, sensor glows once (drawn) | Insert: the mark fills arch by arch | Hold | "Simulated" |
| B5 Settle | 4.60 → 4.60 + `L` | Vignette: Rasheed glances at his phone | Insert: processing, for the real `L`. No cut between tap and banner | Hold | "Simulated" |
| B6 Banner | 5.60–7.40 | Vignette: Rasheed's small pleased nod | Insert: **PAYMENT SUCCESSFUL — PKR 180 RECEIVED**, net PKR 179.19, fee PKR 0.81 and the measured processing time, as the app prints them | Push toward the insert, 1.08→1.15 | UI only |
| B7 SMS | 7.40–10.80 | **Cut** to How-it-works step 4: the hand with the keypad phone buzzes, Rasheed smiling. Then **cut** to prop 6 close-up | On the keypad phone's blank screen, byte-for-byte from the run: *Allied Bank FingerPay: PKR 180.00 paid at Rasheed Kiryana Store via Raast Ref #RAAST-PK-…. ### 2.3 Rules for this loop

- **The SMS shows the shopper's own balance.** It only ever appears on the keypad phone shots, never in a frame with the terminal insert.
- **The Raast reference** comes from the recorded run. Never typed by hand.
- **The "Simulated" tag** (`muted`, label size) appears on every beat showing a thumb capture, settlement or SMS delivery. All three are simulated in the prototype.
- **UI surfaces it needs** (View A):
  - `IDLE` with the presets
  - the amount entered
  - `WAITING_FOR_THUMB`
  - success with net, fee and measured time
  - the store name and Online indicator
- **It also needs** a code-drawn plain SMS text panel sized to the keypad phone's screen.

## 4. Production plan

### 4.1 Art: reuse first, two new vignettes

**Reuse map:** the films use only assets already in `ART-PROMPTS.md`:
- the three character sheets
- props 2 and 6
- the Hero, Problem 3b, How-it-works 2, 3 and 4, For shops, Safety, Every phone and Footer vignettes
- the §4 thumb strip

**New for the pitch film only.** Same process: paste the style block, attach the sheets, generate the key frame first, then a 6 s seamless loop with a locked camera.

- **V1, Bilal at the counter (beats 5–6):**
  ```
  [STYLE BLOCK] Locked camera. Medium shot at the kiryana counter. Rasheed stands behind the wooden counter on the right, the scanner pad and the phone-on-stand in front of him. Bilal stands on the left with his backpack on one shoulder and presses his right thumb on the scanner pad; the oval sensor glows softly once; Rasheed glances at his phone and gives a calm, kind small shrug; Bilal nods, unbothered. No wallet, no cash. The pose eases back to the start for a seamless 6 s loop. Hand-drawn animation timing, holds on the key poses, no motion blur.
  ```
- **V2, take cash calmly (beat 10):**
  ```
  [STYLE BLOCK] Locked camera. Medium shot at the kiryana counter. Rasheed glances at his phone on the stand, then calmly reaches out and takes a single folded banknote from a customer's hand entering from the left edge of the frame, and gives a small reassuring nod. The banknote has no readable markings. Seamless 6 s loop, holds on the key poses, no motion blur.
  ```

**Processing:** the same as the site. Take every second frame to get 12 fps, cut out the background, correct the colour to the palette, and export transparent WebP sequences plus a PNG key frame. The films read those same files, so a change to one art asset updates both the site and the films.

### 4.2 Steps

1. **Before anything:**
   - `brand/BRAND.md` vectors, with the mark split into one path per arch
   - `TYPE-AND-COLOR.md` and `MOTION.md`
   - the approved character sheets and vignettes (the art is the critical path)
2. **Figma storyboards** for every beat, using the approved key frames. One style frame per ratio: first frame, key moment, end card. **User approves.**
3. **Build the merchant app screens** (`APP.md`, SPEC M2):
   - View A: `IDLE`, amount entered, `WAITING_FOR_THUMB`, success, DECLINED_LOW_BALANCE, DECLINED_LIMIT, DECLINED_THUMB_LOCKED, offline
   - View B: the ledger
   - the demo finger tray
   - for beats 8–9: View D's Reply LOCK and View E (M3). The fallback is to drive `api_sms_reply` and show the `test:db` output.
4. **Record a real demo run** on a staging demo deployment (`FP_DEMO_MODE=true`, `FP_ENV=staging`, never production):
   - **Sequence:** seed → Ayesha PKR 180 → Bilal PKR 180 → Bilal PKR 500 → Bilal PKR 2,500 → Ayesha PKR 80 → Ayesha replies LOCK → Ayesha tap (locked) → offline toggle.
   - **Capture:** OBS at 60 fps in a clean browser profile with no URL bar, bookmarks, notifications or devtools. The terminal prompt is anonymised for the `test:db` shot.
   - **Export display fields only** to a fixtures file: outcome codes, amounts, fee, net, first name and initial, `raast_ref`, measured times, SMS bodies, last-4 values. No tokens, keys, CNICs, full phones or account numbers. Login screens and `.demo-credentials.local.md` are never on screen.
5. **Build compositions** in a separate `video/` package (Remotion with React):
   - It reads the processed WebP sequences and the fixtures.
   - It imports the app's real View A and B components with the Astryx theme, so colours and type come from tokens, never hex.
   - Art frame = `floor(frame / 2)` at 24 fps.
   - It is not part of the Next.js bundle.
6. **Render stills:** the first frame, every action, every transition midpoint and the last frame, per video and ratio. **User reviews.**
7. **Script lock:** the user approves the script, then the voice is recorded. Get word timings by transcription, then retime.
8. **Rough cut, then final. User approves each.** Then mix, captions (WebVTT and SRT, plus a burned-in version), encodes, covers and QA (§1.11). Hand off to the website build.

### 4.4 What the user supplies

- **Art:**
  - the approved character sheets
  - the `ART-PROMPTS.md` vignettes as MP4 plus PNG key frames, at the generator's largest size
  - V1 and V2, for the full-length film
- **The voice:** their own recording (a quiet room, 48 kHz), or approval of a specific synthetic voice and its rights.
- **Approvals:**
  - the script
  - the storyboards
  - the stills
  - the rough cut
  - the final
- **Music:** a choice and its licence, or "voice only".
- **Submission specs:** maximum length, format, size limit, and whether it plays in a room or online.
- **Partner status** (SPEC §14 #1). Until confirmed, the wording stays "proposed partners".
